# OUTLINE — The Shared Bridge

Target: 3,500–5,500 words · English body · scene → want/obstacle → turn

## Beats

1. **Open — Warm machine (function 1: caretaking heat)**  
   Tuesday morning, Bay Area coworking kitchen. June descales / fills the scratched kettle. Hiss, wet-coffee-and-vinegar smell, warm metal under thumb. Others take cups, light thanks. WeChat group pings with emoji gratitude. She notes who didn’t rinse.

2. **Want**  
   She wants the shared tools to simply work — without asking anyone, without looking needy. Competence as shelter. Quiet ledger starts: who used, who left grounds, who said “下次我来.”

3. **Group board**  
   Shared Notion “Community Ops” has one assignee: June. Poll for coffee rotation dies at three thumbs-ups. Eric Tan: “Legend!!” Sophie almost claims Thursday, then unclaims after a client call.

4. **Mid — Scalding (function 2: heat as score)**  
   Same week, machine runs hot. June burns a knuckle on the kettle lip while rinsing someone else’s cup. The burn is small; the count in her head is not. She almost posts a sharp note; softens it to a tip. Nobody reads past the emoji.

5. **Obstacle / pressure**  
   Founder mixer wants “who runs your shared stack?” June is the only one with the Notion password, the vinegar schedule, the filter brand. She likes being necessary more than she likes the coffee. A Slack from the space manager: machine flooded once when she skipped a day for an investor call — they assume she’ll handle it.

6. **Stop (turn begins)**  
   She doesn’t announce. She just doesn’t come for three mornings. Buys cafe coffee downstairs. Group chat: polite panic, vinegar poured wrong, sticky notes: “We’ll rotate!!” Kettle left cold under unpaid invoices / sticky pad (function shift).

7. **Return — Cold evidence (function 3)**  
   She finds the scratched kettle cold, used as a paperweight. Cleans once more. Leaves descaling powder + steps. Removes herself from Notion owner without a speech. Walks out with a paper cup.

8. **Cost ending**  
   On the sidewalk she almost replies to three clumsy volunteers with corrections. Types. Deletes. The cost is the identity of holding the bridge — and letting it sag without her narration. No theory. No moral.

## Sensory triad checkpoints

- Open: hiss/steam · vinegar-coffee · warm metal
- Mid: fridge hum / cup knock · burnt grounds · scald on knuckle
- Close: silent cold kettle · stale grounds + paper glue smell · cold scratched steel
