# CHARACTERS — 参差卡

## June Zhou (protagonist)

Solo founder, B2B ops tooling. Bay Area. Speaks English at the coworking desk; Chinese in the WeChat group “Bay Kitchen 湾区灶台.” Ships fast. Rarely asks.

**3 virtues (drive plot)**  
1. **Follow-through** — finishes descaling, docs, filter orders when others flake.  
2. **Situational care** — notices empty water tank, dead Notion permissions, someone’s cup left in the sink.  
3. **Restraint in public** — won’t blow up the group; edits sharp drafts down to tips.

**3 flaws (drive plot)**  
1. **Stingy with asking** — treats need as a product bug in herself; won’t assign the rotation out loud.  
2. **Quiet scorekeeping** — kindness accrues as a private ledger; every “Legend!!” is logged.  
3. **Identity fused to usefulness** — being the only one who knows the password feels like love; giving it up feels like erasure.

参差: the same follow-through that keeps the kettle clean also keeps her from ever saying “I can’t do Tuesday.” Defect carries the stop and the deleted reply.

## Eric Tan

Friendly free-rider. Always first to emoji. Never on the vinegar roster. Not a villain — just light. His lightness makes June’s ledger heavier.

**Virtue:** warmth in chat · **Flaw:** assumes someone competent will always absorb the cost.

## Sophie Wen

Newer founder. Almost helps. Client call wins. Represents the almost-volunteer — not evil, not reliable.

**Virtue:** intention · **Flaw:** intention without calendar weight.

## Kevin (WeChat admin)

Posts polls. Loves process theater. Never touches the machine.

**Virtue:** keeps the group alive · **Flaw:** confuses a poll with labor.

## Space manager (unnamed / “Alex” on Slack)

Assumes June owns kitchen ops because she always has. Institutional free-riding with a smile.
