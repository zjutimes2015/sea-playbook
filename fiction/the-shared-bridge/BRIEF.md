# BRIEF — The Shared Bridge (Track B short-fiction pilot)

Date: 2026-11-04 (America/Los_Angeles)  
Pipeline: amazon-kdp-pipeline · Track B · chang-canghai-drill  
Seat: 写作

## Demand (paraphrased complaints — not invented quotes)

Readers of short fiction online (Reddit writing / Kindle Unlimited short lit threads / diaspora creator chats) keep hitting the same walls:

1. **AI-slop affect**: stories that stack empty emotion nouns (*lonely, desperate, heartbroken*) with no sound, smell, or touch on the ground.
2. **Flat cast**: hero vs villain; no flawed humans whose virtues and defects drag the plot.
3. **Moralizing endings**: the last page explains the lesson (especially free-rider / collective-action) instead of turning and costing something.

## Fire test

Would a tired indie builder / immigrant professional finish this on a phone at 11pm after standup — without hype, without a manifesto?

**Answer:** Yes, if the kitchen and WeChat group feel like Tuesday, the coffee machine changes under the hand three times, and the ending makes her lose a role she liked more than she admits.

## Go criteria (locked)

| Gate | Status |
| --- | --- |
| One concrete situation | Bay Area coworking kitchen + WeChat group of solo Chinese founders abroad |
| One uneven protagonist | Ships product; stingy with asking; kindness that becomes quiet scorekeeping |
| Object/image changes function 3× | Scratched metal kettle / coffee machine path: warm care → scalding ledger → cold evidence |
| Ending costs something | She gives up being the only bridge-holder; does not lecture free-rider theory |

## Niche note (KDP short lit / literary short)

- Not webnovel 爽点; literary/uneven (张氏参差).
- Reader: solo founder, phone, post-standup.
- Adjacent shelves: literary short story, immigrant/diaspora fiction, workplace realist short — not “startup motivational.”
- Pilot aim: craft + pipeline proof, not launch-week ads.

## Decision

**GO.**

Why: the situation is concrete, the protagonist’s defect (scorekeeping kindness) *is* the plot engine, the kettle can carry three temperatures/functions without metaphor lectures, and the free-rider echo can stay in scenes instead of a closing sermon. Matches the demand gaps above and the fire test.
