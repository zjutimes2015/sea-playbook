## Description (HTML-friendly for KDP)

Paste into KDP description; adjust line breaks if the form strips tags.

```html
<p><b>Inspiration is a seed. A complaint is evidence. A payment is a verdict.</b></p>

<p><i>Complaints, Not Inspiration</i> is Book 1 of <b>The Sea Playbook</b>—a practical English field manual for indie developers, overseas builders, and small product teams who ship without a research department.</p>

<p>If your discovery process still looks like a wall of sticky notes, this book replaces photogenic brainstorming with a portable discipline:</p>

<ul>
<li>Build a <b>Complaint Ledger</b> that separates meme weather from structured signal</li>
<li>Run a <b>Fire Test</b> for must-have vs nice-to-have before anyone estimates endpoints</li>
<li>Write a clear <b>jobs-to-be-done</b> one-liner instead of persona incense</li>
<li>Chain pain → wallet weather → competitor scars → tech window—with <b>open-source honesty</b></li>
<li>Sell air first: a priced pre-sale / page before heavy build</li>
<li>Finish with a <b>one-week complaint sprint</b> and a dated kill / revise / proceed verdict</li>
</ul>

<p>You get templates, worked composites, gatekeeper review format (risks before conclusions), and failure modes that show where the method breaks.</p>

<p><b>What this book does not promise:</b> no guaranteed MRR, ranking, or revenue tier. Handbook teaching cases mentioned inside (including approximate repair-path and thin-page traffic shapes) are illustrations for judgment practice—not forecasts for your product.</p>

<p>When demand work has a verdict, continue with Book 2: <i>Ship the Smallest Thing</i>.</p>
```
