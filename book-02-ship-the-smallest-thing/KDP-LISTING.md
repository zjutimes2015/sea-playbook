## Description (HTML-friendly for KDP)

Paste into KDP description; adjust line breaks if the form strips tags.

```html
<p><b>Launch is feedback, not a graduation gift. The homepage promise is the backlog. Pipes are not a business.</b></p>

<p><i>Ship the Smallest Thing</i> is Book 2 of <b>The Sea Playbook</b>—a practical English field manual for indie developers, overseas builders, and small product teams who already have a demand verdict and are about to overbuild.</p>

<p>If your “launch plan” still looks like a cathedral, this book replaces readiness theater with a portable discipline:</p>

<ul>
<li>Treat <b>launch as the start of a feedback loop</b>, not a graduation gift</li>
<li>Choose a stack with a <b>validation clock</b>, not a personality (templates, hosts, open-source honesty)</li>
<li>Let the <b>homepage promise be the backlog</b>: one completable core task, visible and indexable</li>
<li>Make the <b>address work</b>: Git-to-host deploys, domain, mailbox, crawlable HTML</li>
<li>Keep infrastructure <b>explainable</b>: isolation, first-touch attribution, bounded expensive verbs</li>
<li>Treat <b>payment as an asset</b>: buyer, entity, tax role, net proceeds, and trust before the cheapest rate</li>
<li>Prove that the <b>entitlement lands</b> (pay, replay, fail, refund) and write a switch playbook before you depend on one pipe</li>
<li>Finish with a <b>two-week ship-and-collect sprint</b> and a dated ship / shrink / wait verdict</li>
</ul>

<p>You get templates, worked composites, gatekeeper review format (risks before conclusions), and failure modes that show where shipping small breaks.</p>

<p><b>What this book does not promise:</b> no guaranteed launch time, conversion, approval, payout, or revenue tier. Handbook teaching cases mentioned inside (including approximate fee-table impressions, a small abuse-bill shape, and platform dramas) are illustrations for judgment practice—not forecasts for your product.</p>

<p>Best read after Book 1: <i>Complaints, Not Inspiration</i>. When the loop is observable, continue with Book 3: <i>Buy Time, Not PMF</i>.</p>
```
