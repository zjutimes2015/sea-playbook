```html
<p><b>Indexing is not a business. Being seen is not being chosen. Being linked is not being recommended.</b></p>

<p><i>Earn the Click</i> is Book 4 of <b>The Sea Playbook</b>—a practical English field manual for indie developers, overseas builders, and small product teams who already have a channel plan and are about to confuse an indexed URL with a living product.</p>

<p>If your “organic plan” still looks like a page factory, a green coverage badge, or a purchased mention, this book replaces search theater with a portable discipline:</p>

<ul>
<li>Treat <b>storage, rank, visits, and finished jobs</b> as four different events</li>
<li>Work search as a <b>system</b>: crawl, then index, then rank; the page and the query are the unit</li>
<li>Build a <b>keyword-to-page map</b> that survives demand, competition, monetization, and effort—not a volume sort</li>
<li>Let the <b>crawler finish the job</b>: inspect the HTML a machine actually received</li>
<li>Publish <b>increment, not inventory</b>, and run a last-click test before you request indexing</li>
<li>Treat answer-engine work as <b>eligibility</b>: lift-able sentences, honest structure, probes that record omission and error</li>
<li>Diagnose <b>links as trust</b> in five steps before you ask for a mention; quality is context, not a count</li>
<li>Finish with a <b>two-week search-and-citation sprint</b> and a dated keep / prune / deepen / diagnose-first verdict</li>
</ul>

<p>You get templates, worked composites, gatekeeper review format (risks before conclusions), an interlude on tool-page types and the factory temptation, and failure modes that show where earning the click breaks.</p>

<p><b>What this book does not promise:</b> no guaranteed indexing, ranking, citations, referring domains, conversion, or revenue tier. Handbook teaching cases mentioned inside (including an index-rate shape, an organic-traffic shape, and a second-hand editing experiment) are illustrations for judgment practice—not forecasts for your site.</p>

<p>Best read after Book 3: <i>Buy Time, Not PMF</i>. When the search verdict is honest, continue with Book 5: <i>Numbers That Decide</i>.</p>
```
