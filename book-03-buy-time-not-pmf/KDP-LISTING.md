<p><b>Own one or two channels. Ads buy time, not fit. A click-through rate is not a market.</b></p>

<p><i>Buy Time, Not PMF</i> is Book 3 of <b>The Sea Playbook</b>—a practical English field manual for indie developers, overseas builders, and small product teams who already have a completable product loop and are about to confuse attention with product-market fit.</p>

<p>If your “growth plan” still looks like a five-platform parade, this book replaces distribution theater with a portable discipline:</p>

<ul>
<li>Treat a <b>click-through rate as a doorbell</b>, not as a verdict on the job</li>
<li>Map traffic into <b>rented, paid, and owned pools</b>, and settle visits into a hook you control</li>
<li><b>Own one primary channel and at most one auxiliary</b>—the map is not a to-do list</li>
<li>Treat <b>cold start as membership</b>, not a launch party; communities are not ad slots</li>
<li>Build the <b>pool you actually own</b> (email and product hooks; referrals only when deserved)</li>
<li>Use <b>ads as a clock</b>: official coupon first, tight geography, intent phrases, a written ceiling</li>
<li>Prove that <b>tracking can accept a purchase</b> (value and currency) before you spend to learn</li>
<li>Finish with a <b>two-week channel-and-clock sprint</b> and a dated keep / switch / pause / fix-the-journey verdict</li>
</ul>

<p>You get templates, worked composites, gatekeeper review format (risks before conclusions), and failure modes that show where buying time breaks.</p>

<p><b>What this book does not promise:</b> no guaranteed visits, ranking, click-through rate, conversion, payout, or revenue tier. Handbook teaching cases mentioned inside (including an ads spend/revenue shape in Hong Kong dollars with API cost excluded, a community campaign with clicks and zero orders, recall counts, and publisher-path sketches) are illustrations for judgment practice—not forecasts for your product.</p>

<p>Best read after Book 2: <i>Ship the Smallest Thing</i>. When the channel plan is honest, continue with Book 4: <i>Earn the Click</i>.</p>
