## H. How This Appendix Relates to the Series End

These cards are the portable end of Book 5 and of the planned five-book path. They do not open a sixth title. If a later experiment needs a new product, it needs a new Book 1 fire test, not a new souvenir chart.

When you publish, if you publish, you will still need a human calibre pass and a human KDP upload. This Appendix will not do that work for you.
